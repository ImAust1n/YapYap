Capabilities

What the engine does

Six tightly-coupled modules that work together to turn raw dictation into production-ready text.

🎙
Speech-to-Text
Integrates with local Whisper or Vosk STT engines. Raw transcription is piped directly into the cleaning pipeline without any cloud round-trip.
Local STT
🧹
Filler Removal
Automatically detects and removes filler words like "um", "uh", "like", "you know", "basically", and locale-specific fillers such as "matlab".
Rule-based NLP
🔄
Repetition Detection
Uses a sliding window algorithm to detect consecutive repeated words and phrases, collapsing them into a single occurrence seamlessly.
N-gram Matching
✏️
Grammar Correction
Applies deterministic grammar rules — capitalization, punctuation inference, subject-verb agreement — without any LLMs or external APIs.
Zero LLM
📄
Auto Formatting
Groups clauses into coherent sentences and paragraphs. Infers sentence boundaries from pause durations and syntactic signals.
Sentence Segmentation
🎨
Tone Control
Switch between Formal, Casual, Concise, and Neutral modes. Each mode applies a post-processing pass that adjusts vocabulary and sentence structure.
4 Modes
